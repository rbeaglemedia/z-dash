Hold the phone however is comfy, then tap START. Let go of THROTTLE to coast and slow down. That's your level: all tilt is from there. Re-center any time in ⏸.

HOW TO PLAY

🛫You start on the RUNWAY. Hold THROTTLE and build speed: no lift until ROTATE speed (the green mark).
🚪WINGS start CLOSED (no lift, any speed). Tap WINGS to open the doors + hatch. Open early = gentle takeoff; stay closed, build speed, then open = FAST LAUNCH.
⬆️When the bar says PULL UP! tilt the top of the phone toward you to lift off.
↔️Tilt left/right to BANK and turn. Away = dive (gain speed), toward = climb (trade speed for height).
🌀Tilt HARD left/right for a BARREL ROLL. Climb too steep and slow and she STALLS: let the nose drop, then pull up.
💨Hold NOS for a burst. The bottle drains while held and refills on its own.
🧈Land soft: slow down, nose a little up, AIR BRAKE. Float it in under 6 mph for a BUTTER LANDING!
🛞On the ground, hold AIR BRAKE to slow in a straight line. Slide that finger right and an E-BRAKE pad pops up under it. Lift or slide back off the pad and it vanishes. Hold THROTTLE with it to spin donuts.
Desktop: hold E = throttle · F = wings · arrows/WASD · double-tap ←/→ or Z/C = barrel roll · Shift = NOS · Space = air brake · drag right off AIR BRAKE = E-BRAKE pad
