How many more items
36 fps budget is 27.8 ms. Indoor spare is about 11 ms on phone and PC and about 4 ms on the A10 stand-in.
A 0.05 ms floor (well above the measured blit) leaves room for about 80 more world sprites on the stand-in and about 200 on phone and PC.
Room props are inside the one-shot bake, so they do not add that per-frame cost.
Safe catalog step: 5 more rows, one per category, then measure again.
Do not preload every category. The stand-in menu is in the 36-55 hold band (cap stays 8, scale stays 1).
Outdoor frames on this VM are the city renderer. The cap drop to 4 is that cost. A phone already near 57 fps outdoors should keep cap 12 and scale 1.
